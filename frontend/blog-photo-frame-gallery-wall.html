How to Style a Photo Frame Gallery Wall at Home

A step-by-step guide to planning a gallery wall with photo frames: layout, spacing, mixing sizes and choosing photos.

A gallery wall lets your family photos and favourite prints live together as one display. It looks complicated, but with a plan it is an easy weekend project.

1. Choose a theme for the photos

Pick a thread: black and white portraits, travel pictures, festivals, or one family over the years. A theme makes mixed sizes look deliberate.

2. Plan on the floor first

Lay your frames on the floor in the shape of the wall space. Start with the largest frame near the centre and build outwards.

3. Mix sizes, keep one thing the same

Use different sizes but keep either the frame colour or the mat colour consistent. That one repeated detail keeps the wall tidy.

4. Keep the spacing even

Leave about 5 cm between frames. Even gaps matter more than perfect symmetry.

5. Hang from the middle outwards

Fix the centre frame first, then work outwards. Use a spirit level for the first few frames and the rest will follow.

6. Leave room to grow

Leave space at the edges. A gallery wall is nicer when you can add the next trip or celebration to it.

Quick tip: Print photos with a little extra border so nothing important is hidden behind the frame edge or mat.
