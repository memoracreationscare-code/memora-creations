Diwali Home Decor Ideas: Simple Ways to Light Up Your Home

Easy Diwali and festive home decor ideas: entrance, living room, pooja corner, lighting and gifting.

Festivals are when a home feels most alive. You do not need to change everything for Diwali. A few focused touches at the entrance, the pooja corner and the living room do the most.

1. Start at the entrance

Your door is the first thing guests see. A fresh toran, a rangoli and a neat nameplate set the mood before anyone steps inside.

2. Refresh the pooja corner

Clean the space, add fresh flowers and place a few diyas on a tray. A small decorative backdrop makes the corner feel special.

3. Layer your lighting

Mix diyas, string lights and a few lanterns at different heights. Warm light looks best; avoid too many colours at once.

4. Update cushions and throws

Changing cushion covers to festive colours like maroon, mustard or deep green gives a new look at a low cost.

5. Decorate with a table centrepiece

A decorative bowl, a floral arrangement or a candle set on the dining or centre table keeps the festive feel close to where people sit.

6. Plan your gifts early

Order gifts for family and friends a week or two ahead so they arrive on time, and add a personal message to each one.

Quick tip: Use candles or diyas only where they are safe. Keep them away from curtains and always away from children and pets.
