7 Easy Living Room Wall Decor Ideas on a Budget

Simple, affordable ways to make an empty living room wall feel finished: frames, mirrors, shelves, colour and lighting.

An empty wall can make a whole room feel unfinished. The good news is you do not need a big budget or an interior designer. A few thoughtful pieces, placed with care, can change how your living room feels.

1. Start with one focus wall

Pick the wall people see first when they enter, usually the one behind the sofa. Decorating one wall well looks better than decorating all four halfway.

2. Use frames in a group

A set of three to five frames in matching or complementary finishes looks organised and personal. Keep the gaps between frames equal, about 5 to 7 cm, so the group reads as one piece.

3. Hang art at eye level

The centre of a picture should sit around 145 cm from the floor. Over a sofa, leave about 20 to 25 cm between the sofa top and the bottom of the frame.

4. Add a mirror for light

A mirror opposite a window bounces daylight around and makes a small room feel bigger. A round or arched shape softens a room full of straight lines.

5. Mix in shelves and small objects

A floating shelf with a vase, a small plant and one or two keepsakes adds depth. Odd numbers of objects usually look more natural than pairs.

6. Let colour do some work

A single accent colour repeated in a frame, a cushion and a vase ties the room together. Stay with two or three colours so the wall does not feel busy.

7. Finish with warm lighting

A warm wall light or a table lamp nearby makes your decor glow in the evening. Choose warm white bulbs rather than cool white for a homely feel.

Quick tip: Before you drill, cut paper to the size of each frame and tape it to the wall. Move the paper until the layout feels right, then mark the nail positions.
