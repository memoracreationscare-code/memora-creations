Products CSV se jodo

Excel ya Google Sheet me products likhkar CSV me save karein aur yahan daalein. Bahut saare products ek saath ban jate hain.

Columns (pehli line me ye naam hone chahiye):
name description category originalPrice sellingPrice stock keywords (optional, comma se alag)
category ka naam wahi likhein jo Products page par hai.
Products chhupe hue (draft) bante hain, kyunki photo CSV se nahi aati. Products page par har product me photo lagakar "Website par dikhao" dabayein.
