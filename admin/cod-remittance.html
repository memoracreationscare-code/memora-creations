COD Remittance

Where the money of your Cash on Delivery orders is: with the courier, waiting to reach your bank, or already received. Amounts are what the customer pays. Shiprocket deducts shipping and COD charges before it pays you, so your bank credit will be a little lower.

Check the real payout in Shiprocket: log in to Shiprocket and open Billing → COD Remittance. It shows each payout, its date and the exact amount sent to your bank. This page does not read that list, it only shows what your own orders say.
Mark as received is only for your record: press it after the money has reached your bank. It does not move any money.
The “expected” dates assume about 8–9 days after delivery, which is Shiprocket’s usual time. Your plan may be different.
